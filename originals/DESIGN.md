## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, safe, and joyful outdoor adventure ecosystem tailored for children, families, educators, and donors. Built upon an ethos of active discovery, community care, and wholesome optimism, the visual personality balances structured modern minimalism with exuberant warmth. 

The aesthetic fuses crisp white space, high-readability typographic hierarchies, and organic, friendly pill geometry. Rather than chaotic or juvenile clutter, the interface uses bright nature-inspired accents—warm coral, lime grass, sunny gold, and sky blue—grounded against crisp neutral surfaces and rich slate text. The resulting emotional tone is welcoming, dependable, and brimming with kinetic vitality.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uplifting, sunlit atmosphere engineered strictly for WCAG AA compliance and effortless comprehension across generations.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#F08080` - Light Coral):** The nurturing emotional core. Used for hero interaction points, vital donation CTA banners, and signature interactive badges.
- **Secondary (`#32CD32` - Lime Green):** Outdoor growth and active play. Applied to secondary CTAs, positive status badges, progress bars, and nature-centric highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Sky Blue):** Open horizons and clarity. Applied to informational highlights, tags, and accent borders.
- **Accent Yellow (`#FBBF24` - Sunny Gold):** Warmth and celebration. Reserved for impact milestones, star ratings, and celebratory alerts.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate):** Anchors all body copy, primary labels, and structural typography, guaranteeing high contrast and readable cadence against light containers.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure, clean canvas creating an unencumbered visual backdrop. Secondary surfaces utilize `#F8FAFC` to subtly compartmentalize secondary blocks.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`):** Whisper-thin boundaries maintaining crisp structural boundaries without adding visual heaviness.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate balance between playful geometry and effortless legibility. 

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides structural character for display, headline, and label roles. Its wide apertures, geometric balance, and curved junctions convey safety, forward motion, and warmth without appearing childish.

**Nunito Sans** handles body narrative and dense editorial blocks. Its softened humanist terminals and open counters make it exceptionally gentle on the eye across long reading sessions, perfect for stories, mission statements, and event information.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system that flexes seamlessly across screen widths:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 40px outer page margin, with max content constraint capped at 1280px for optimal focal length.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 28px page margin.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px page margin.

Vertical spacing relies strictly on a standardized 8pt geometric rhythm. Large sections utilize generous airy offsets (`space-2xl`) to keep layouts calm and clutter-free, allowing young learners and parents to focus cleanly on one action or story at a time.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a clean, airy, sun-drenched outdoor atmosphere, this design system avoids heavy shadows, dark silhouettes, or stark skeuomorphic drop-offs. Visual depth is established through a combination of crisp pure-white surfaces, low-contrast ghost borders, and radiant ambient undertones:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` flat baseline.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Pure white container accompanied by a crisp 1.5px border in `#F1F5F9` and a soft ambient glow: `0px 6px 20px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Floating Elements):** Lifted surfaces transition upward with `0px 12px 28px -6px rgba(240, 128, 128, 0.12)` or `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`, offering responsive, tactile feedback.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.14)` combined with an ultra-light frosted scrim (`backdrop-blur-sm` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The shape system embraces **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry, expressing child-friendly safety, community approachability, and organic softness. 

- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Used universally for action buttons, filter tags, interactive pills, and status chips.
- **Extra Large Containers (`rounded-xl` / 48px):** Applied to major hero panels, donation highlights, and showcase modules.
- **Large Components (`rounded-lg` / 32px):** Applied to standard interactive cards, forms, event modules, and media wrappers.
- **Small Elements (`rounded` / 16px):** Inputs, tooltips, and compact dialog boxes.

Corners are consistently ultra-soft to eliminate sharp tension and reinforce tactile safety across the experience.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`), solid `#F08080` background, pure white text (`label-lg`), zero border, with subtle hover lift and color shift to `#E57373`.
- **Secondary Button:** Solid `#32CD32` or `#38BDF8` background with crisp white typography, used for secondary action triggers (e.g., "Join a Camp", "Explore Activities").
- **Outline Button:** Pill shape with a 2px outline in `#F08080` or `#1E293B`, transparent interior, shifting to a 10% tinted fill on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Program & Category Chips:** Pill containers featuring soft pastel tinted fills (e.g., `#F08080` at 12% opacity) paired with high-contrast text (`#1E293B` or full-strength accent hex).
- **Status Indicators:** Pill badge featuring a 6px vibrant colored dot beside crisp uppercase microcopy (`label-sm`).

### Cards
- **Activity & Impact Cards:** Set in `#FFFFFF` with a 32px (`rounded-lg`) border radius, bounded by a 1.5px border (`#F1F5F9`), and surrounded by our Level 1 ambient glow. Top image headers use matching inner rounded corners and maintain a clean 16:10 aspect ratio.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pill-shaped (48px height) or 16px rounded rectangular form fields with a `#FFFFFF` fill, 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, and deep slate typography. Focused states highlight with a 2px vibrant coral outline (`#F08080`) and an ambient glow ring (`rgba(240, 128, 128, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smoothly rounded squares (`8px` radius) and circles (`9999px`) featuring a 2px outline in `#94A3B8`. Checked states fill with `#32CD32` and feature an energetic white checkmark.

### Additional Non-Profit Domain Components
- **Donation Tier Selector:** Pill button group with custom amount input fields. Selected amounts trigger a cheerful `#FBBF24` (Sunny Gold) or `#F08080` outline with high-contrast active states.
- **Fundraising Progress Bar:** 16px tall track with full pill rounded ends in `#F1F5F9`. Filled progress is rendered in vibrant `#32CD32` with an optional sunny gold pulse at the leading edge.
